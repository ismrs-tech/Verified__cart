## 🎯 What is VerifiedCart?

VerifiedCart is a **zero-hallucination grounded shopping assistant** that:
- **Never invents prices** — every number is copied verbatim from a 5,000-product catalogue snapshot
- **Explicitly refuses** out-of-catalogue queries (iPhones, groceries, medicines) instead of guessing
- **Clarifies ambiguity** by presenting clickable disambiguation options
- **Cites every claim** with `[SKU-XXXX]` or `[§Policy Section]` references
- **Understands Hinglish** — *"sabse sasta 1.5L kettle jo stock me ho"* works perfectly
- **Multi-turn memory** — tracks your budget and preferences across conversation turns

## 🏗️ Architecture: 4-Stage Zero-Hallucination Pipeline

```
User Query (English / Hinglish)
        ↓
[Stage 1] Hinglish & Intent Normalizer
  • Translates: "sabse sasta" → cheapest, "kitne ka" → price
  • Extracts: budget limits, superlatives, stock requirements
  • Resolves: brand synonyms (e.g. "krida" → "Kridaa")
        ↓
[Stage 2] Inverted Index & Policy Matcher
  • Fast lexical retrieval over 5,000 SKUs
  • Exact section-level policy matching (§1.1 to §8.1)
  • Sub-millisecond response time
        ↓
[Stage 3] Tri-State Decision Gate
  • ANSWER   — single/exact match found
  • CLARIFY  — 2+ ambiguous matches, show options
  • REFUSE   — out-of-catalogue or policy gap
        ↓
[Stage 4] Verbatim Snapshot Extractor
  • Prices NEVER generated by AI
  • Copied directly from catalogue.csv
  • Every claim carries [SKU-XXXX] or [§Policy] citation
```
